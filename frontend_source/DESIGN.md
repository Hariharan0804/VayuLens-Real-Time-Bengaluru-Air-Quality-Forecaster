## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an atmosphere of serene, high-fidelity environmental intelligence. Built to reframe civic atmospheric telemetry from clinical dread into restorative agency, the experience pairs rigorous algorithmic forecasting with natural, grounded warmth. It rejects the hyper-saturated blues, neon accents, and harsh cyberpunk dashboard motifs common in technical SaaS, leaning instead into an earthy, organic tactile-minimalist aesthetic. 

The aesthetic draws inspiration from natural history editorial archives, fine horticultural field guides, and contemporary architectural environmental design. Whitespace is expansive and meditative, instilling clarity in high-density sensor networks. Surfaces feel dry, warm, and sunlit, evoking the garden city roots of Bengaluru through textural paper tones, deep mossy canopies, and warm clay highlights. Visuals prioritize pure abstract sensor geometries, vector atmospheric isobar ribbons, and precision typographic meters over generic figurative photography.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly around natural pigments—terracotta kiln dust, dried sage leaves, dense canopy shade, and sun-dried paper.

- **Primary Background (`#F7F3EA`)**: A grounded, warm ivory base with subtle amber undertones. It eliminates eye fatigue and sets an inviting, organic tone.
- **Card Background (`#FFFDF8`)**: A warm porcelain white that elevates interactive layers softly above the ivory base without harsh starkness.
- **Primary Green (`#294236`)**: Deep forest pine providing dependable structural grounding, high-contrast typography, and primary interactive weight.
- **Secondary Green (`#718A6B`)**: Sage leaf green applied for positive environmental readings, secondary CTAs, active sensor status pips, and serene metric visualizations.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#DCCDB5`)**: Warm sand tone for track lines, dividers, secondary tags, and subtle contextual chip fills.
- **Warm Accent (`#C87855`)**: Fired terracotta reserved exclusively for acute data calls, PM2.5 threshold warnings, air quality spikes, and focal alerts.
- **Text Primary (`#343832`)**: Warm charcoal stone offering accessible, high-legibility typographic hierarchy without the harshness of pure black.
- **Text Muted (`#73786D`)**: Olive gray tuned for microcopy, metric units, timestamp data, and auxiliary telemetry captions.
- **Border Neutral (`#E6DFD1`)**: Soft, delicate beige outlining structural panels with barely-there physical edge definition.

## Typography
Typographic expression balances editorial authority with algorithmic precision. 

Headings rely on **Newsreader**, an expressive, organic transitional serif that imparts scholarly weight and botanical grace to environmental reports, regional forecasts, and ambient condition summaries. Numerical readouts, micro-telemetry, controls, and body paragraphs rely on **Manrope**, an open, geometric neo-grotesque that preserves pristine readability across technical datasets. 

For dense environmental telemetry (e.g., AQI ratings, PM10 readings, wind vectors), Manrope must be styled with proportional tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain vertical balance across time-series ticker boards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout structure operates on a generous 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by rhythmic intervals of 8px. Outer borders and gutters prioritize breathable negative space, giving data cards the cadence of an architectural field journal.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, 48px canvas margin, 24px gutters. Regional ward lists, macro-atmospheric maps, and hourly bar charts distribute across asymmetrical 8-column and 4-column groupings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, 32px canvas margin, 20px gutters. Lateral analytical sidebars drop into stacked modules below the primary vector map.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column grid, 20px canvas margin, 16px gutters. Spatial elements flow into single-column vertical sequences, using compact horizontal swipe rails for ward-by-ward monitoring.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on physical layering and warm ambient diffusion rather than digital drop shadows or harsh highlights.

1. **Substrate Base**: Canvas color `#F7F3EA` sits completely flat at the lowest tier.
2. **Surface Tier (Cards & Panels)**: `#FFFDF8` panels layered over the substrate with a crisp 1px border of `#E6DFD1`. Ambient shadow is ultra-soft and tinted warmly: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(41, 66, 54, 0.04), 0 1px 4px 0 rgba(41, 66, 54, 0.02)`.
3. **Elevated Overlays (Flyouts & Detail Popovers)**: `#FFFDF8` elevated with a double shadow structure tinted with forest tones: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(41, 66, 54, 0.08), 0 2px 8px -1px rgba(41, 66, 54, 0.04)`, bordered with `#DCCDB5`.
4. **Depth Transitions**: Active states depress physically (a slight downward transform of 1px) rather than flaring outwards with intense glows, underscoring the grounded, analog ethos.

## Shapes
Shapes evoke natural ergonomics—soft, rounded, and pebble-like without losing computational alignment. A uniform baseline radius of `0.5rem` (8px) scales gracefully up to `1rem` (16px) for cards and telemetry modules, creating soft silhouettes reminiscent of architectural ceramic tiles. Interactive toggles and badges embrace continuous pill geometries (`9999px`) to maintain tactile friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#294236` (Forest Green) background with `#FFFDF8` text. Border-radius of `0.5rem` (8px). On hover, color transitions gently to `#718A6B` (Sage Green).
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button utilizing a 1px `#E6DFD1` stroke, `#FFFDF8` background, and `#343832` text. Hover brings a subtle background tint of `#F7F3EA` and border shift to `#DCCDB5`.
- **Tertiary/Urgent Action**: `#C87855` (Terracotta) background with `#FFFDF8` text, reserved for acute safety warnings, export reports, or emergency advisory toggles.

### Air Quality Metric Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFDF8` surface fill, `1rem` corner rounding, and 1px `#E6DFD1` borders.
- Structured with high internal padding (`1.5rem`). Metric index numbers are rendered in `data-metric` Manrope with tabular tracking, flanked by a discreet status indicator dot.
- Sub-data indices (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, Humidity) feature subtle `#DCCDB5` dividing rules and `#73786D` micro-labels.

### Status Chips & Ward Badges
- Continuous pill radius (`9999px`). 
- **Optimal Air State**: Faded sage wash (`rgba(113, 138, 107, 0.12)`) with `#294236` text and an internal filled 6px circle of `#718A6B`.
- **Advisory/Polluted State**: Muted terracotta wash (`rgba(200, 120, 85, 0.12)`) with `#C87855` bold text.
- Micro-padding: 4px vertical by 10px horizontal.

### Charts & Isobar Forecast Graphs
- Clean vector line graphs with non-solid fills: transparent gradient fading from `#718A6B` (Sage) into `#FFFDF8` with 0% opacity.
- Axis lines rendered in thin 1px `#E6DFD1` with dotted intervals. Labels rendered in 11px Manrope `#73786D`.
- Tooltips are mini-cards in `#FFFDF8` with a 1px `#DCCDB5` frame, casting an ultra-soft ambient shadow.

### Inputs & Ward Selectors
- `#FFFDF8` background with an inset 1px `#E6DFD1` border and 8px corner radius.
- Text rendered in `#343832` with `#73786D` placeholders.
- Focus state softens the ring into an organic dual ring: a crisp 1px `#718A6B` stroke enveloped by a 3px diffused outer halo of `rgba(113, 138, 107, 0.20)`. No bright cobalt or harsh blue focus halos.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squares for checkboxes (4px radius) and concentric circles for radios.
- Unchecked: `#FFFDF8` with a 1.5px `#DCCDB5` border.
- Checked: `#294236` fill with an ivory `#FFFDF8` check icon or dot.

### Lists & Sensor Matrices
- Separated with hairline dividers of `#E6DFD1`.
- Hovering a regional row (e.g., "Whitefield", "Indiranagar", "Jayanagar") highlights the container with an ultra-soft wash of `#F7F3EA`, smoothly transitioning the leading text to `#294236`.
